HOW TO PLAY

Hackers are trying to break into your account! Your password is your first layer of protection, but passwords alone are not always enough.

Multifactor Authentication (MFA) adds another verification step to help protect your account, even if someone knows your password.

YOUR MISSION

  • Move using A/D or the arrow keys.
  • Press SPACE to shoot incoming hackers.
  • Collect the green A for an authenticator app.
  • Collect the blue K for a security key.
  • Activate MFA to unlock extra defenses.
  • Press E to use your MFA defensive pulse.
  • Answer security questions between waves.
  • Defeat the final boss!

Remember: A password is only your first line of defense. Add another factor to make your account more secure!
